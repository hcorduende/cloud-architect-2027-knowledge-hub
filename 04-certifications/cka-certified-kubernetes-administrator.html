CKA — Certified Kubernetes Administrator

Build practical Kubernetes administration and troubleshooting capability.

Study domains

Cluster architecture; installation and configuration; workloads and scheduling; services and networking; storage; troubleshooting.

Hands-on preparation

Operate a Kubernetes cluster: deploy workloads, configure probes and services, manage persistent volumes, inspect events and troubleshoot failures.

Exam readiness

Practice timed kubectl tasks and verify you can diagnose issues from manifests, events, logs and cluster state.
