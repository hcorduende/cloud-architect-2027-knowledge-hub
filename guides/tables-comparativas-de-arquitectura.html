Architecture Comparisons

A decision aid for comparing cloud architecture options by requirements and trade-offs.

Comparison dimensions

Availability and recovery; security and identity; performance and scalability; operational complexity; portability; cost; governance and compliance.

How to use

Start with business and non-functional requirements. Compare options against measurable criteria, state assumptions and document why the chosen design is appropriate.

Examples

Availability Zones vs multi-region; managed Kubernetes vs serverless containers; VPN vs private connectivity; relational vs NoSQL data stores.
