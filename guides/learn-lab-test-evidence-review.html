LEARN → LAB → TEST → EVIDENCE → REVIEW

A repeatable learning loop that converts study time into demonstrable competence.

Five stages

LEARN: understand the concept and prerequisites. LAB: implement it hands-on. TEST: validate behavior and security. EVIDENCE: capture code, diagrams and results. REVIEW: document lessons and update the knowledge base.

Definition of done

A topic is complete when you can explain it, implement a working example, validate expected outcomes, share sanitized evidence and identify remaining gaps.

Quality gate

Prefer small, reproducible labs. Tear down billable resources. Never commit secrets or confidential information.
